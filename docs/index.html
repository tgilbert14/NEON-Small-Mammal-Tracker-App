What you can do

Pick a site on the map

The landing is a national map of every NEON site — dot size is how many animals were caught, color is the dominant mammal family. Tap a dot to dive in.

Explore by species

Flip to “by species” and the map redraws to show where one animal turns up across the country — a live range map for any of 150+ species.

Read the community

Sex & age structure, a Hill-number diversity profile, body-size distributions, and breeding phenology — the whole community for a site & window.

Detection-corrected abundance

Closed-capture estimates (Schnabel / Chapman) that count the animals the traps missed, shown alongside Minimum-Number-Known-Alive with honest caveats.

Follow one animal

Every ear-tag becomes a life story — a trap-grid home-range heatmap and replay, a capture history, and a shareable holographic trading card.

Compare & report

Put two sites head-to-head, take a 30-second guided tour, or print a one-page site report card to PDF.

Honest by design

  • Abundance leads with transparent indices — MNKA (minimum known alive) and catch-per-effort — before any model.
  • The detection-corrected estimate is hidden when within-bout recaptures are too few (< 3) to be stable, and clamped to never fall below the count actually handled.
  • Diversity uses Hill numbers over distinct individuals, so a much-recaptured animal isn't counted twice.
  • The “chonk” score is an honest adult weight percentile within species — not a dressed-up condition index that would just rank measurement noise.
  • Rarity tiers track trappability & residency, not ecological rarity — and we say so.
