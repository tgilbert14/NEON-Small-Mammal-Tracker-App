What this can tell you

Within-site capture activity, community composition, tagged-animal histories, MNKA, and supported detection-corrected estimates—with their sampling qualifications.

What this cannot tell you

Absolute density from CPUE, a fair raw-count contest between biomes, universal rainfall effects, or causation from a short observational record.

Methods & data

Trust the denominator.

A source row may be an empty trap, a handling record, or one animal in a multi-capture event. The tracker resolves physical opportunity before it calculates catch per effort.

Sampling unitA reviewed physical trap event at site, plot, coordinate, and night—not every source row.
EffortExact field-status rules assign 0, 0.5, or 1 trap-night; reviewed multi-captures collapse and two documented double traps remain two.
OpportunitySpecies CPUE uses the site's full reviewed trap opportunity, including traps that did not catch that species.
MissingnessBlank weight or hind-foot values mean unmeasured—not zero. Ambiguous keys and unreviewed duplicate patterns fail closed.
UncertaintyDetection-corrected abundance is gated, intervalled, and never allowed below the minimum number known alive.
